Green tests alone prove nothing

Pass rate

How often generated tests pass. It is necessary, but not sufficient.

Mutation score

Seed known bugs into a branch of the portal, such as a wrong total or broken validation. Count how many the tests catch.

The mutation score is the honest measure of autonomous QA. A regression check against the hand-written facturación cases comes next.

The pass rate can look great while the tests check nothing. The mutation score is what catches that.
