Rules that stop it becoming a behemoth

  • One contract per stage. JSON in, JSON out, tested alone.
  • n8n orchestrates, nothing more. No business logic in workflow nodes.
  • Per-project differences live in a profile. Never in code.
  • Models do bounded jobs. Their output is always validated.
  • Independent oracle. Expected behavior comes from human requirements, never the code.
  • Human gates. A person approves cases and reviews every PR. No auto-merge.
These rules are why the project can grow. Pick one and ask who would break it first.
